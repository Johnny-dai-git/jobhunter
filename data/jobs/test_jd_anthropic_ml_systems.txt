About the Role

Responsibilities

- Develop high-performance inference serving systems with sub-100ms latency requirements

- Design observability tooling for GPU workloads, network telemetry, and training stability
- Collaborate with research teams on novel parallelism strategies (TP/PP/FSDP/sequence parallel)

Required

- Strong Python and C/C++ skills
- Deep experience with PyTorch (DDP/FSDP), Megatron-LM, or similar distributed training frameworks
- Familiarity with Kubernetes-based ML platforms
- Experience profiling and debugging at the systems level (network, kernel, GPU)

Nice to Have
- Experience with vLLM or other LLM serving frameworks
- Publications at top systems/ML venues (MLSys, NSDI, SOSP, OSDI)
- eBPF / kernel tracing background
- Experience with NCCL internals
- Knowledge of GPU architecture (CUDA, NVLink, RDMA)
